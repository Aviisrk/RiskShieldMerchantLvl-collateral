Appendix B · Methodology

How this was computed.

Windowed to the last 180 days of transaction date; disputes joined on their originating transaction.

Dispute rates (§5.1)

Amount rate = disputed ÷ captured amount — the primary rate this desk verdicts on, because enforcement and recovery here are amount-driven. Count rate = disputes ÷ captured (SUCCESS) transactions — kept as a compliance reference, matching how card networks monitor. Both are labelled wherever shown.

Benchmarks (§5.2, as of 2026-07-01)

Visa VAMP (merchant): above-standard 0.5%, excessive 1.5% (count-based, effective 2026-04-01; minimum 1,500 settled txns to action). Mastercard ECP: ECM ≥ 100 chargebacks and 1.5% ratio; HECM ≥ 300 and 3% (lagged (prior-month) chargeback-to-transaction ratio). UPI per-MCC peer percentile: no benchmark available. Sources: Visa Acquirer Monitoring Program, 2026 thresholds; Mastercard Excessive Chargeback Program, 2026.

Eligibility & ratio capping (§5.3)

Identifiers rank by disputed amount and must clear captured ≥ ₹5,000 or ≥ 3 txns or ≥ 2 disputes. Ratio = min(disputed, captured) ÷ captured, capped at 100%; rows where disputed > captured are marked ▲ and listed in Appendix D.

Verdict engine (§5.4)

Five signals scored 0–100, each graded independently, then weighted: amount dispute-rate 30%, fraud-coded share 25%, rate trend 20%, concentration 15%, network / known-bad 10%. The amount rate is scored against an internal amount band (above-standard 1%, excessive 3%) because no card network monitors an amount-basis rate; count-basis VAMP/ECP are shown alongside as a compliance reference. Grade bands: LOW < 20, GUARDED < 40, ELEVATED < 60, HIGH < 80, CRITICAL ≥ 80. The verdict line reconciles to its top drivers.

Clustering, reasons, spikes (§5.5–5.7)

VPA handles collapse by stripping a trailing numeric suffix. Reason codes map to groups: 1401/128 → fraud; 1403/1064/1065/1061 → service; 1084 → processing; 4853 → conditional cardholder dispute; unmapped codes raise a data-quality alert. A week is a spike when its amount dispute-rate exceeds max(3× the trailing-12-week median, 1.0%) and disputed value ≥ ₹25,000.

Appendix C · Glossary

Terms.

Amount dispute rate
Disputed value divided by captured value. The primary rate this desk verdicts on — enforcement and recovery here are amount-driven.
Count dispute rate
Disputes divided by captured (successful) transactions. Kept as a compliance reference — the rate card networks monitor.
PAR
Payment Account Reference — a stable card identity that survives re-issuance, used to rank card sources.
VPA
Virtual Payment Address — a UPI handle such as name@bank.
CGNAT
Carrier-grade NAT (100.64.0.0/10): shared address space where many users sit behind one IP.
DCH
Data-centre-hosted IP — traffic from hosting providers rather than consumer connections.
Chargeback-to-transaction ratio
Chargebacks divided by transactions, the basis for card-network monitoring programmes.
Preventable (strict)
Disputes on identifiers already on a blacklist, DOT-MNRL list, or with a prior dispute before the transaction.
Over-index
A payment mode’s dispute share divided by its GMV share; above 1.0× means more disputes than its volume warrants.
